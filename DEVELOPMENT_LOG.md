## 2026-10-22 — Physical KDL photograph and HDA crash analysis

- User supplied a photo of the X270 in Kernel Debugging Land. It shows `vm_page_fault: unhandled page fault in kernel space at 0x8`, thread `audio_capture`, during `open()` of `/dev/audio/hmulti/hda/0`. The faulting module is the installed non-packaged `hda`, symbol `reset_controller(hda_controller*) + 0x1f9`, reached from `hda_hw_init` and `hda_open`.
- `HDAC_GLOBAL_CONTROL` is register offset `0x08`; `reset_controller()` reads it through `controller->regs`. A fault at address `0x8` is consistent with `controller->regs == NULL`. The photograph does not by itself prove why that pointer became null.
- Source review found a plausible race: `hda_open()` checked `opened` then initialized hardware before incrementing it; `hda_close()` decremented `opened` before `hda_free()` called `hda_hw_uninit()`, which unmaps registers and sets `regs = NULL`. The observed sequence followed killing media servers and opening the capture device, which could overlap cleanup and re-open. This is stronger evidence for an HDA lifecycle race than the earlier USB suspicion as the immediate cause of the hang.
- Prepared local Haiku branch `x270-hda-selector` commit `f943506`: reserve `opened` atomically before initialization, release it on init failure or after `hda_hw_uninit()` in `free()`. `git diff --check` passed. **Not built or deployed.** The selector fix is its parent commit `3cb094c`.
- After physical restart, first collect logs and verify boot/network. Keep the patched HDA override out of service before any further audio capture; test the lifecycle fix only after compiling on the X270 and retaining the packaged driver as fallback.
